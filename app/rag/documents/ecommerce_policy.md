# ShopEase Customer Policy

## Returns

### Eligibility

#### 30-day return window
Most items, including clothing, shoes and footwear, home goods and accessories, can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The return window is counted from the date the order was delivered, not the date it was placed. Returns requested after the 30-day window has passed are not accepted, except for damaged or defective items covered under the Damaged Items or Warranty policies.

#### Electronics return window
Electronics (such as earbuds, smartwatches, chargers and other devices) have a shorter return window of 15 days from delivery. Electronics must be returned with all accessories, manuals and original packaging.

#### Condition requirements
Returned items must be unused, unwashed and in their original condition with tags attached. Footwear must be returned in the original shoe box and must not show signs of outdoor wear. Items returned in an unacceptable condition may be sent back to the customer without a refund.

#### Order status requirement
A return can only be started for an order that has been delivered. Orders that are still processing can be cancelled instead. Orders that are in transit or delayed cannot be returned until they have been delivered.

### Non-returnable items

#### Non-returnable product categories
The following items cannot be returned or exchanged: swimwear and intimate apparel (for hygiene reasons), gift cards and digital gift cards, opened personal care and beauty products (such as serums, creams and cosmetics), and perishable goods. Items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase are also non-returnable.

### How to return

#### Return process
To return an item, request a return through customer support or your account with your order number and the item you want to return, and give a reason for the return. Once the return is approved you will receive a prepaid return shipping label by email. Pack the item securely in its original packaging and drop it off with the carrier within 7 days of receiving the label.

#### Return shipping costs
Return shipping is free for damaged, defective or incorrect items. For all other returns, a flat return shipping fee of $5.99 is deducted from the refund.

## Damaged and defective items

### Reporting damage

#### Damaged on arrival
If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the item and the packaging. Damaged items qualify for a free replacement or a full refund including original shipping costs, regardless of category.

#### Wrong item received
If you receive an item that is different from what you ordered, report it within 14 days of delivery. We will send the correct item at no extra cost and provide a prepaid label for the incorrect item.

## Refunds

### Refund processing

#### Refund processing time
Refunds are processed and issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse. After the refund is issued, banks and card issuers typically take an additional 3-5 business days to show the credit on your statement.

#### Delayed refunds
If a refund still has not arrived or been received more than 14 calendar days after it was initiated, the case should be reviewed by a support specialist. A refund that has not arrived within that period is not yet considered delayed. Support agents cannot promise a specific refund date beyond these processing times.

#### Cancelled orders
If an order is cancelled before it ships, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 3-5 business days of the cancellation.

### Refund amounts

#### What is refunded
Refunds cover the price paid for the returned item. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error (damaged, defective or incorrect item). Partial refunds may be issued for items returned used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts.

## Exchanges

### Exchange eligibility

#### Size and color exchanges
Items eligible for return can be exchanged for a different size or color of the same product within the 30-day return window, subject to availability. If the replacement is out of stock, a refund is issued instead.

#### Exchange limits
Each item can be exchanged once. Non-returnable items cannot be exchanged. Exchanges for a different product are handled as a return followed by a new order.

## Warranty

### Coverage

#### Standard warranty
Electronics and home appliances carry a 1-year limited manufacturer warranty from the delivery date, covering defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.

#### Warranty claims
To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number, a description of the defect and photos or video. Eligible items are repaired or replaced; if repair or replacement is not possible, a refund may be offered at the discretion of the warranty team. Warranty claims are reviewed by a support specialist.

## Shipping

### Delivery times

#### Standard delivery
Standard delivery takes 3-7 business days after the order ships. Orders are usually shipped within 1-2 business days of being placed.

#### Delayed shipments
Carrier delays can occur due to weather, high volume or regional disruptions. If a shipment is more than 5 business days past its estimated delivery date, customers may request a support review. Estimated delivery dates are provided by the carrier and are not guaranteed.

#### Lost packages
If tracking has shown no update for 10 or more days, the package may be treated as lost. Lost packages are investigated with the carrier and, if confirmed lost, replaced or refunded in full.

### Order changes

#### Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they are still processing. Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the return policy.

## Privacy and security

### Account protection

#### Order information access
Order details are only shared with the account holder who placed the order. Support will never share another customer's order, tracking, payment or personal information.

#### Suspected fraud
Suspected fraud, unauthorised account access or payment disputes are always handled by a human support specialist.

## FAQ

### General questions

#### How do I track my order
You can ask support for your order status using your order number. Tracking details include the carrier, tracking number, current location and estimated delivery date.

#### What is your return policy
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, and electronics within 15 days, as long as they are unused and in their original condition. Some items, such as swimwear, gift cards and opened personal care products, cannot be returned. Once your return is received and inspected, the refund is issued to your original payment method.

#### How long does a refund take
Refunds are issued 5-7 business days after your return is received and inspected, and your bank may take a further 3-5 business days to show the credit.

#### Can I speak to a person
Yes. You can ask to speak with a human support agent at any time and your case will be handed over with a summary so you do not need to repeat yourself.

#### Do you ship internationally
Currently we only ship within the country. International shipping is not available.
